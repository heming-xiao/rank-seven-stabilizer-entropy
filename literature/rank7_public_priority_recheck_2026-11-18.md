# Rank-seven public literature recheck, 2026-11-18

The target is the global minimum of the order-two stabilizer Rényi entropy on the complex local-unitary orbit of a pure state, for every Schmidt rank-seven spectrum and every qubit bipartition. The candidate minimizer assigns the Schmidt coefficients in decreasing order to matching computational labels. A counterexample to that prescription on the same domain would also resolve the target question negatively.

| Primary source reviewed | Scope relevant to the priority check |
| --- | --- |
| [Sierant, Spectral geometry of nonlocal stabilizer entropy, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13106v2#S2.SS2.SSS2) | The latest public arXiv revision is dated September 14. The global theorems cover arbitrary Schmidt spectra through rank six and spectra satisfying the dyadic-staircase conditions. Section II.2.2 explicitly leaves rank seven and higher outside that family open. Its rank-seven projector compression estimate is a proof ingredient. |
| [Liu and Cui, Entanglement, anti-flatness, and nonlocal nonstabilizerness, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01993) | The ordered Schmidt representative is a local minimum for integer orders at least two. The paper also gives bounds and spectral asymptotics. |
| [Torre, Franchini and Giampaolo, Non-Local Magic from the Entanglement Spectrum](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.07808) | Exact formulas in the ordered Schmidt gauge, including its Walsh–Hadamard representation. Section on the Schmidt gauge identifies equality with the local-orbit optimum as a conjecture. |
| [Franchini and Giampaolo, Nonlocal magic and the symmetries of quantum systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02745) | Exact Schmidt gauge for a one-qubit versus rest partition, where the Schmidt rank is at most two. |
| [Sierant, Exact quantification of nonlocal magic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28563) and [Viscardi, Leone and Hamma, Non-local Magic, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04119v2) | The closed-form local-orbit result is for stabilizer fidelity or its negative logarithm. |
| [Jha et al., Nonlocal magic of fermions](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07003v1#S3.SS2) | Proposition S4 gives Gaussian optimality for up to three BCS pairs among parity-preserving local unitaries. The product-labeled BCS candidate and fermion parity restriction are both part of the result. |
| [Akella, Tripartite nonlocal magic](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13071v1) | The introductory sentence about exact bipartite results cites Sierant's fidelity paper, Torre et al.'s Schmidt-gauge calculation, and Viscardi et al.'s fidelity paper. Its main theorem concerns tripartite invariants. |
| [Wu, Entanglement distillation, nonlocal magic, and state sharing](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21490v1) | Many-copy asymptotics for a specific binomial Schmidt spectrum, using spectral shell bounds and their bounded optimization gap. |
