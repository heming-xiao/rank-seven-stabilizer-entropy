# Uniform packing prices for the cut containing the first and either middle direction

November 18, 2026. A closed six-ray spectral test applies to an arbitrary
ordered rank-seven spectrum \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma,a,b,c,d)\) in the cone
\(\gamma d>ab,\ \alpha cd>\beta ab\). Evaluating the test requires elementary
arithmetic and one square root of each of two scores. Its finite packing
certificates are common to all spectra. Both strictly ordered spectra
proportional to \((19.32,4.69,4.13,1.012,1.008,1.004,1)\) and
\((15,4.2,4,1.03,1.02,1.01,1)\) pass with strict margins.

Set \(h=\alpha-\beta,\ k=\beta-\gamma,\ q=\gamma-a,\ m=\alpha-a\),
\(g=hq,\ \Delta=km,\ F=a m(k+q)q\). For a single fixed complex frame,
let \(v=L_{01}-2,\ \nu=L_{01}+L_{02}-4,\ n=\nu_+,\ r=v_+\).
The trace of the selected cut \(\mathcal C=\{I:0\in I,\ I\cap\{1,2\}\ne\varnothing\}\)
is \(s=\sum_{I\in\mathcal C}w_I=3+\nu+x\), where
\(x=1-\sum_{i=3}^6w_{012i}\ge0\). Write \(Y=U_1\) for the weighted
highest-pair difference and \(U=U_1+U_2\) for its shared counterpart, always
against the sorted computational reference. \(E\) denotes the normalized
linear exterior gain. [Definitions of the probes](rank7_general_two_probe_matching.md)

## Uniform trace price on the positive-gain branch

The original forward gap estimate uses the two prices \(A=gcd,\ B=\Delta d^2\).
It strengthens to
\[
 E\le A\nu+Bv-m(k+q)qD_A\le A\nu+Bv-Fx,\qquad
 D_A=a-\sum_{i=3}^6 t_i w_{012i}.
 \tag{1}
\]
In the nonnegative forward gaps \(b_j=t_j-t_{j+1}\), \(b_6=d\), retain all
quartic terms with exactly three indices in \(\{0,1,2\}\) and the fourth
index in \(\{3,4,5,6\}\). The three small indices can only be assigned to
actual wedge labels \(0,1,2\). Their combined coefficients are generated by
the product \((\alpha-a)(\beta-a)(\gamma-a)\); the remaining factor is the
fourth tail probability. Thus their deficit is precisely the third term
of (1). These monomials are disjoint from the five exceptional ones of
the original forward certificate. Finally \(D_A\ge ax\) by positivity.
[Forward certificate](rank7_forward_coefficients_independent.md)

The aggregate of the four directed rows containing \(0\) and missing one
of \(3,4,5,6\) gives \(2\nu+x\le1\). Indeed its cost before dropping
the nonnegative three-tail contribution is \(3(1-x)+2(s-1+x)\),
plus that contribution, and its capacity is eight. Hence \(x\ge
2(s-3)-1\). Using \(\nu=s-3-x\) and \(v\le1/3\) in (1), positive gain
forces
\[
 s-3<\frac{A+F+B/3}{A+2F}\qquad(A+2F>0).
 \tag{2}
\]
Define \(\omega=(F-B/3)_+/(A+2F)\), taking \(\omega=0\) if the
denominator vanishes. The minimum score inside the cut is
\(\alpha\gamma cd\) and the maximum outside is
\(\max(\alpha abc,\beta\gamma ab)\). The two cone inequalities make
the amplitude gap
\[
 \sigma=\sqrt{\alpha\gamma cd}-
             \sqrt{\max(\alpha abc,\beta\gamma ab)}
\]
positive. The one-cut compression bound is
\(\mathcal D_F\ge\sigma^2\{s\}(1-\{s\})\). In the case \(\omega>0\),
(2) gives \(s<4-\omega\). If \(3\le s<4-\omega\), the fractional trace
product is at least \(\omega(s-3)\); for \(s<3\) one uses nonnegativity.
As \(s-3=\nu+x\), every positive-gain frame therefore satisfies
\[
 \mathcal D_F\ge\sigma^2\omega(s-3)_+\ge\sigma^2\omega n .
 \tag{3}
\]

## One packing comparison for two continuous weighted prices

For \(0\le\lambda\le g/2,\ 0\le\mu\le\Delta/2\), set
\[
 M=\max(n,r/2),\quad Z=\max(5n/2,2r,2n+r),\quad
 D=d[m(k+q)q-\lambda(k+2q)-\mu q].
\]
Here \(D\ge0\): at the upper allowed prices the bracket is
\(q(k+q)(k/2+q)\). Put \(\theta=\min(1,D/(d^3q))\) when \(q>0\)
and zero otherwise, and \(H=3M+\theta(2n+r-3M)_+\). Then every frame
at every sorted positive spectrum, including those outside the cut cone,
satisfies
\[
 \boxed{E-\lambda U-\mu Y\le
  (g-\lambda)cd\,\nu+(\Delta-\mu)d^2v
  -d^3[(h+2k)M+qH]-d^4 Z.}
 \tag{4}
\]

First set forward gaps \(b_3,b_4,b_5\) to zero, keeping
\(b_0=h,b_1=k,b_2=q,b_6=d\). The resulting auxiliary probabilities are
\((m+d,k+q+d,q+d,d,d,d,d)\). Let \(N=\sum_Iw_I\), let \(T_i\) be the
total occupancy of wedges containing \(i\), and \(V_{12}=L_{12}-2\).
Write \(\delta\) for actual minus reference. Expanding this flat-tail
quartic as a product with common floor \(d\) gives the constant-occupancy
term \(d^4\delta N\), the singleton terms
\[
 d^3\left[h\,\delta T_0+k\,\delta(T_0+T_1)+q\,\delta(T_0+T_1+T_2)\right],
\]
the three pair terms with factors \(d^2\) times products of the corresponding
head increments \(m,k+q,q\), and the triple term
\(-d\,m(k+q)q\,x\). The two flat weighted differences are
\(Y_f=d^2v-dqx,\ U_f=d^2\nu-d(k+2q)x\). The three-head pair row
\(V_{12}+\nu\le0\) implies that the pair terms are at most
\(d^2(g\nu+\Delta v)\). The net triple coefficient remaining after
subtracting \(\lambda U_f+\mu Y_f\) is exactly \(-Dx\).

The following geometric prices use only the original 98 rows:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \delta T_0&\le-M,&
 \delta(T_0+T_1)&\le-2M,&
 \delta(T_0+T_1+T_2)&\le-\max(3M,2\nu+v-x),\\
 \delta N&\le-Z .
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
The reference singleton totals are \(4,4,4\) and \(N_*=7\). For example,
the third extra bound is the linear row
\(T_0+T_1+T_2+2(L_{01}+L_{02})+L_{01}+\sum_iw_{012i}\le23\).
Each maximum in (5) comes from separate linear prices. Replacing signed
variables by positive parts is valid in the terms \(2n+r\) by cases:
if \(\nu<0\), the other branches \(3r/2\) for \(H\) and \(2r\) for \(Z\)
dominate \(r\), and if \(v<0\) the branches \(3n,5n/2\) dominate \(2n\).
Moreover \(d^3q\max(3M,2\nu+v-x)+Dx\ge d^3qH\), by
\((z-x)_++\theta x\ge\theta z_+\) for \(x\ge0\), \(0\le\theta\le1\).
This proves the flat-tail estimate.

For the general tails expand \(E-\lambda U-\mu Y\) in forward gaps,
writing \(\lambda=z g,\ \mu=y\Delta\) with \(z,y\in[0,1/2]\).
Of 210 quartics, 175 contain at least one of the restored indices
\(3,4,5\). Subtract also the charge
\((1-z)g d(c-d)\nu\), whose only quartic is \(0256\).
All 175 resulting coefficients have nonpositive actual-minus-reference
difference. For 127, the coefficients are unchanged from the original
forward proof. The remaining 48 have new column costs affine in
\((z,y)\). Four rational physical-row duals per cost certify all four
corners of the square. Convex interpolation supplies the entire square,
including cases \(g=0\) or \(\Delta=0\) via the allowable zero price.
An independent expansion attached to the verifier builds the four-wedge,
\(gU\), \(\Delta Y\), and \(0256\) coefficients separately. Adding the
restored charge to the flat-tail term \((g-\lambda)d^2\nu\) yields
exactly \((g-\lambda)cd\,\nu\), proving (4).

## Six rays with no spectrum-dependent LP

Suppose \(G=g-(200/11)b(c-d)\ge0\), and choose \(\lambda=G/7\),
\(\mu=\Delta/7\). Set
\[
 R=k^2[k^2+(h^2+q^2)/2],\quad
 C_s=\frac{12G^2+g^2}{12\cdot168},\qquad
 C_1=\frac{12\Delta^2+R}{(5/2)\cdot168},\qquad
 C_F=\tfrac47\sigma^2\omega .
\]
On the six directions \((n,r)=(1,0),(1,1/2),(1,1),(1,2),(1,4),(0,1)\)
evaluate \(M,Z,H\) as defined above and test
\[
 \begin{split}
 d^3[(h+2k)M+qH]+d^4 Z+C_s\max(n,r)+C_1r+C_Fn
 \ \ge\ (g-\lambda)cd\,n+(\Delta-\mu)d^2r .
 \end{split}
 \tag{6}
\]
These are sufficient for global optimality of the sorted computational
representative under arbitrary complex local unitaries on every qubit
bipartition. Positive homogeneity reduces the nonnegative \((n,r)\)
quadrant to its directions. The listed rays include all points at which
one of the maxima changes slope: \(M\) at 2, \(Z\) at \(1/2,2\),
\(H\) at \(1,2,4\), and \(\max(n,r)\) at 1 (finite numbers denote \(r/n\)).
Thus testing (6) on six rays proves it throughout the quadrant.

Indeed the known reciprocal mixed-probe bound at shifts \(G,\Delta\)
and the independent anticommuting bound supply at least
\(24G U+24\Delta Y+(12G^2+g^2)\kappa+(12\Delta^2+R)\kappa_1\).
Use \(\kappa\ge\max(n,r)/12,\ \kappa_1\ge r/(5/2)\), and use the
additional \(96\mathcal D_F\) payment from (3) when \(E>0\). Formula
(4) with (6) then pays \(168E\). Nonpositive exterior gain is immediate
from nonnegative actual defects. With strict six-ray margins, compactness
on \(n+r=1\) gives surplus at least a positive constant times \(n+r\).
By (1), \(E\le An+Br\); positive gain therefore leaves a uniform
positive fraction of the sum of actual defects. The strict-margin
and two-frame transfer give a full relative open neighborhood of
optimality at every such spectrum. [Mixed and angular defect bounds](rank7_leading_split_joint_payment.md) · [Strict transfer](rank7_open_interior_neighborhood.md)

The exact checker verifies the three families of geometric support rows
and the \(2\nu+x\) row, the 127 original quartic duals, and all 192
new weighted-corner comparisons. It checks the spectral gaps with rational
square-root enclosures. At the first displayed strictly ordered spectrum
every ray margin exceeds \(3/2\). Formula (6) applies by direct spectral
substitution throughout its parameter region; it is a sufficient condition.
