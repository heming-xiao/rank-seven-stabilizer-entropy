## Six-mode occupation and unused anticommuting slots

Let `P₆` project onto the first six modes, `L=ℬΛ²C`, `z₆=ℬΛ²P₆`, `β₆=2‖z₆‖²` and `κ=2‖u‖²`. The weighted occurrence `W₆` counts wedges among the first six modes containing 01 or 02, counting both when both occur. In the complementary-pair matching on six vertices, an edge on `{3,4,5}` has price two, an edge connecting that set to 1 or 2 has price one, and all other edges have price zero. A matching has at most two positive edges, at most one of price two. Hence `W₆≤3`.

Specializing the exact mixed polarization to `C=P₆` makes the sum of the direct pair terms two. With `p=p₀₁+p₀₂` and `s₆=∑_{i<j<6}pᵢⱼ` it gives `2⟨u,z₆⟩=2+W₆−⟨p s₆⟩`. Thus `⟨p s₆⟩−5=(W₆−3)−2⟨u,z₆⟩≤√(κβ₆)`, with no change of the normalized skew-label average. The formal shifted estimate retains the nonnegative term `K(3−W₆)`, with `K=b(c−d)−ρ≥0` after the one-edge correction. [Exact mixed normalization](../rank7_general_two_probe_matching.md)

Restricting the anticommuting Pauli projection of `P₆⊗P₆` to antisymmetric copies gives `β₆≤2J₆₆`. For any odd cut `S` the paired leakage sum is at least one on every anticommuting Pauli edge. In either term the inner anticommuting Parseval sum for `P₆` is bounded by `6D/2`. Hence `J₆₆≤12J₆S` and `J₆S≥β₆/24`. Let `ε=c−d`. The diagonal slot `2ε⁴J₆₆` and the four slots `4ε² gap(S)² J₆S` for `S=P₁,P₃,P₅,P₇` supply `ε²Ψβ₆`, where `Ψ=ε²+[h²+q²+(b−c)²+d²]/6`. ## Full-spectrum triangle probe and joint allocation

For the same-frame baseline `B=diag(a,a,a,a,b,c,d)`, let `L_B=ℬΛ²B`. The three leading increments are `y=(X,Y,Z)`. Its direct leading-edge increment is `a(yᵢ+yⱼ)+yᵢyⱼ` and its only changed Fano completion is the remaining leading increment paired with vertex 3, adding `a yₖ`. Thus the reference achieves this conditional optimum. With no leading edge, rearrangement gives `aX+bY+cZ`. The last conditional gap is `j₀=YZ+(a−b)Y+(a−c)Z`.

The monotone spectral-gap estimate can be applied successively in the two slots of `J(C,C)`. It preserves the full `2J(B,B)` independently of the old head-only slots and of the head-to-tail slots contained in the cross terms `J(Pᵢ,B)`. The baseline term satisfies `2J(B,B)≥2‖L_B‖²`.
